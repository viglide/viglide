# Code of Conduct

Be respectful and professional. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and
bad-faith participation are not tolerated in issues, PRs, discussions, or any project space.
Disagreements are argued with evidence and civility. Maintainers may edit, lock, or remove
content and ban participants who violate this policy. Report conduct issues privately to
alexander.oreshkevich@gmail.com; reports are handled confidentially.
